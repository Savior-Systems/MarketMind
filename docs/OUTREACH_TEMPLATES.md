# MarketMind Personal Outreach Templates ✉️

This document outlines ready-to-use message templates for direct outreach via DM (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Discord, Telegram, or email) to contacts, colleagues, and developer networks ahead of launch day.

---

## Template 1: Casual DM to Friends & Colleagues
*Use this for close friends, former coworkers, or general tech acquaintances.*

```text
Hey [Name]! 

I've been secretly building something for months — an open-source AI marketing platform that replaces Buffer, Jasper, and Hootsuite for $0/month.

I'm launching it publicly on [LAUNCH DATE]. 

Would mean the world if you could star the repo when it goes live. No pressure — just excited (and terrified) about launch day. 🚀

I'll send the link on [date]. Thanks for even reading this! 🙏
```

---

## Template 2: Technical DM to Developer Networks
*Use this for software engineers, developers, system administrators, or open-source contributors.*

```text
Yo [Name]! 

Built something you might find interesting — an open-source AI agent swarm for marketing automation. Python/FastAPI, LangGraph, ChromaDB. Runs on a $5 VPS with Docker.

Launching public on [DATE]. Would love your star and honest code review on GitHub. Especially the agent orchestration layer — would value your opinion on it.

Will send the link. 🤙
```

## Template 3: DM to Content Creators & Marketers
*Use this for writers, designers, growth marketers, or startup founders who manage active social feeds.*

```text
Hi [Name]! 

I built a free, self-hosted AI tool that generates and schedules social media content across all platforms. It replaces Buffer, Jasper, and Hootsuite combined.

Launching it open-source on [DATE]. If you're curious, I'd love for you to check it out and star the repo.

No subscription. No paywall. Just free forever.

Sending the link on [date]! 💜
```
